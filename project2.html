The Problem

Initial builds suffered from frame-rate dependent movement variations. On higher refresh rate monitors, the physics calculation caused the character to jump higher or fall faster, breaking level balance.

The Kinetic Solution

Implemented a deterministic physics loop utilizing Time.fixedDeltaTime coupled with a custom interpolation layer. This ensured that regardless of rendering FPS, the movement vector remained consistent within 0.001% variance across all hardware configurations.
